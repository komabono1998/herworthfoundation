Who we are

Rooted in Gulu, led by youth

We are a youth-focused community-based organisation working to help girls, women and youth reach their full potential. Since 2021 we have reached more than 10,500 girls, women and boys across northern Uganda.

Gulu Menstrual Hygiene Run
Annual event

Run for menstrual health

Our yearly Gulu Menstrual Hygiene Run raises awareness and advocates for menstrual health. Proceeds fund reusable pads for girls in northern Uganda.
