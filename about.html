About us

Helping girls, women and youth reach their potential

A youth-focused community-based organisation in Gulu, northern Uganda, founded in 2021.

🎯

Our mission

To empower women and girls with resources, education and support to thrive.

🔭

Our vision

A world where every woman realises her worth and achieves her dreams, with women, girls and youth inspired, empowered and resilient.

Our reach

10,500+ people since 2021

We have reached over 10,500 girls, women and boys. About three in four are female and one in four are male, because ending period poverty is a community effort that includes boys and men.

75% female · 25% male
