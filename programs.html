Our thematic areas of work

Practical programmes that keep girls healthy, confident and in control of their futures.

SRHR advocacy & menstrual health

Sexual and reproductive health and rights advocacy, with a focus on menstrual health.

  • Reusable sanitary pads for girls
  • Pad-making training
  • Menstrual hygiene management training
🧭

Life-skills training

Skills that help girls and young people make informed decisions and build self-reliance.

🌟

Leadership & mentorship

Mentorship that connects young women with role models and opportunities to lead.

🌍

Climate change

Involving youth in understanding and responding to climate change in their communities.
