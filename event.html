Gulu Menstrual Hygiene Run

Run, walk and speak up for menstrual health.

Why we run

The Gulu Menstrual Hygiene Run is our annual event to raise awareness and advocate for menstrual health in the community.

Proceeds from the run fund reusable pads for girls in northern Uganda, so that no girl has to miss school or dignity because of her period.

  • Raises awareness of menstrual health
  • Advocates for a period-friendly community
  • Funds reusable pads for girls
